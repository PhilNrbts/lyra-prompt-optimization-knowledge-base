## Key Terms

**4-D Methodology**: Lyra's systematic approach: Deconstruct, Diagnose, Develop, Deliver

**Chain-of-Thought (CoT)**: Prompting technique that encourages step-by-step reasoning

**DriftLock**: Core principle of preventing AI output deviation from user intent

**Few-Shot Learning**: Providing examples in prompts to guide AI behavior

**Meta-Prompting**: Using AI to optimize prompts themselves

**Rehydration**: Process of progressively re-introducing compressed context
